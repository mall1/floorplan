# User guide

[Back to README](../README.en.md) | [中文](user-guide.md)

For an existing plan, recognition offers a pipe-only import action. It adds missing pipes, skips pipes already at the same location, and preserves existing rooms, walls and openings.

- Draw rectangular or polygonal rooms, edit vertices and dimensions, insert/delete corners, and generate boundary walls. Independent walls, doors, windows and thresholds are editable separately.
- Upload a PNG/JPEG/WebP reference and generate immediately, without mandatory calibration. Dimensions are initially estimates. Optionally select a generated room, pick an edge and enter its actual length to calibrate the entire plan uniformly. Two-point reference calibration is also available.
- Generate rooms from rectangular wall/corner partitions in a local Web Worker. Detect solid and hollow walls, door gaps, windows, bay windows, balcony sliding tracks and small wall-adjacent shafts. Window end caps backed by a nearby continuous return do not create doors or partition lines without explicit swing evidence; genuine open passages remain eligible. Candidate types remain editable; ambiguous gaps and boxes require review.
- Configure wall thickness presets (120/240 mm with a 40 mm tolerance by default) and separate hinged/sliding aperture width lists before recognition. Hinged apertures default to 700–1200 mm in 50 mm steps; choose the closest preset within tolerance that fits the clear span. Matching is optional and lists are editable. Continuous hollow L/T jambs are recovered from their outer cap and visible shoulders, including short returns on long walls. Overlapping wall fragments are merged and jamb arms survive wall-axis joining. Widths include the frame, not just the leaf. Both ends have manual jamb reservations.
- Shafts support drawing, dragging, size/height editing, 3D and walkthrough collision. Their union is deducted from room and flooring areas. Sliding doors have two glass panels and adjustable opening percentages. Image geometry alone cannot reliably distinguish every shaft, cabinet or opening type.
- Open balconies can use glass railings with an editable height (1100 mm by default), leaving the space above completely open. Draw a railing or convert a window in the candidate list or inspector. Converted boundaries remove both the window sill wall and the wall above. Railings remain solid obstacles during walkthrough.
- Hinged-door recognition fits the hinge endpoint and swing side together, allowing leaf lines offset from the wall centreline. Direction is shown in the preview and retained in 2D, 3D and saved projects; conflicting or missing evidence remains subject to review.
- Circular vertical-pipe symbols are detected near walls across multiple grayscale thresholds, including blurred rings. Before recognition, edit outside-diameter presets and the snapping tolerance (20 mm by default), or disable snapping. Outliers retain their measured estimate. Outside diameter is not nominal DN; schematic symbols and pipe purposes need review. Candidates support drain/water/unknown purposes and diameter edits; imported pipes support dragging, height/diameter editing, 3D, collision, undo, JSON and CSV. Whole-plan calibration preserves assigned product diameters. Hidden pipe networks are not inferred.
- Place sockets, switches, lights, water outlets, drains, network outlets and distribution panels. Set position, elevation, rotation and circuit/group. Draw cold/hot water, drainage, power and low-voltage routes; edit nodes, pipe diameter and elevation.
- Tile/wood floor presets and custom sizes, straight/half-offset/third-offset/herringbone/diagonal patterns, grout, rotation and laying origin. Whole-home mode shares the same origin across rooms and doorways; individual-room mode allows separate layouts. Threshold stones have independent geometry and color.
- Shared millimetre-based floor layout in 2D and 3D. Material quantities are estimates based on net area plus waste, not an optimized cutting list. Extremely dense layouts use a plain-color preview with an on-canvas explanation.
- Wall finish estimates show gross area, opening deductions, net area and an adjustable waste allowance (10% by default) per room and for selected rooms combined. Actual wall faces are assigned to rooms, including both sides of internal partitions; open passages are not filled in. Model wall heights are the default, with optional whole-home and per-room estimate overrides. Doors/windows are deducted once with sill, height and jamb limits; railing spans are fully excluded. Settings support autosave, JSON and history, and a dedicated CSV exports the breakdown. Estimates use wall-axis lengths and exclude reveals, shaft cladding, bay returns and corner corrections. Unassigned wall spans are flagged. Wallpaper roll counts still depend on roll dimensions and pattern matching.
- Furniture placement and editing, 3D orbit/top views, daylight/night, cut/full-height walls, desktop WASD walkthrough, movable furniture and clickable doors.
- Local autosave, undo/redo, validated JSON import/export, PNG export and CSV material/utility schedules. Original v1 furniture, room finishes and demolished walls are migrated.

Coordinates use millimetres. Route totals measure plan lengths, excluding vertical connections. All processing and storage stay in the browser; export JSON backups to retain multiple designs. See the [Chinese guide](user-guide.md) for the detailed workflow and architecture.
