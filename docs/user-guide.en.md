# User guide

[Back to README](../README.en.md) | [中文](user-guide.md)

For an existing plan, recognition offers a pipe-only import action. It adds missing pipes, skips pipes already at the same location, and preserves existing rooms, walls and openings.

- Draw rectangular or polygonal rooms, edit vertices and dimensions, insert/delete corners, and generate boundary walls. Independent walls, doors, windows and thresholds are editable separately.
- Upload a PNG/JPEG/WebP reference and generate immediately, without mandatory calibration. Dimensions are initially estimates. Optionally select a generated room, pick an edge and enter its actual length to calibrate the entire plan uniformly. Two-point reference calibration is also available.
- Generate rooms from rectangular wall/corner partitions in a local Web Worker. Detect solid and hollow walls, door gaps, windows, bay windows, balcony sliding tracks and small wall-adjacent shafts. Window end caps backed by a nearby continuous return do not create doors or partition lines without explicit swing evidence; genuine open passages remain eligible. Candidate types remain editable; ambiguous gaps and boxes require review.
- Configure wall thickness presets (120/240 mm with a 40 mm tolerance by default) and separate hinged/sliding aperture width lists before recognition. Hinged apertures default to 700–1200 mm in 50 mm steps; choose the closest preset within tolerance that fits the clear span. Matching is optional and lists are editable. Continuous hollow L/T jambs are recovered from their outer cap and visible shoulders, including short returns on long walls. Overlapping wall fragments are merged and jamb arms survive wall-axis joining. Widths include the frame, not just the leaf. Both ends have manual jamb reservations.
- Shafts support drawing, dragging, size/height editing, 3D and walkthrough collision. Their union is deducted from room and flooring areas. Sliding doors have two glass panels and adjustable opening percentages. Image geometry alone cannot reliably distinguish every shaft, cabinet or opening type.
- Open balconies can use glass railings with an editable height (1100 mm by default), leaving the space above completely open. Draw a railing or convert a window in the candidate list or inspector. Converted boundaries remove both the window sill wall and the wall above. Railings remain solid obstacles during walkthrough.
- Hinged-door recognition fits the hinge endpoint and swing side together, allowing leaf lines offset from the wall centreline. Direction is shown in the preview and retained in 2D, 3D and saved projects; conflicting or missing evidence remains subject to review.
- Circular vertical-pipe symbols are detected near walls across multiple grayscale thresholds, including blurred rings. Before recognition, edit outside-diameter presets and the snapping tolerance (20 mm by default), or disable snapping. Outliers retain their measured estimate. Outside diameter is not nominal DN; schematic symbols and pipe purposes need review. Candidates support drain/water/unknown purposes and diameter edits; imported pipes support dragging, height/diameter editing, 3D, collision, undo, JSON and CSV. Whole-plan calibration preserves assigned product diameters. Hidden pipe networks are not inferred.
- Place sockets, switches, lights, water outlets, drains, network outlets and distribution panels. Set position, elevation, rotation and circuit/group. Draw cold/hot water, drainage, power and low-voltage routes; edit nodes, pipe diameter and elevation.
- Tile/wood floor presets and custom sizes, straight/half-offset/third-offset/herringbone/diagonal patterns, grout, rotation and laying origin. Whole-home mode shares the same origin across rooms and doorways; individual-room mode allows separate layouts. Threshold stones have independent geometry and color.
- Shared millimetre-based floor layout in 2D and 3D. Material quantities are estimates based on net area plus waste, not an optimized cutting list. Extremely dense layouts use a plain-color preview with an on-canvas explanation.
- Wall finish estimates show gross area, opening deductions, net area and an adjustable waste allowance (10% by default) per room and for selected rooms combined. Actual wall faces are assigned to rooms, including both sides of internal partitions; open passages are not filled in. Model wall heights are the default, with optional whole-home and per-room estimate overrides. Doors/windows are deducted once with sill, height and jamb limits; railing spans are fully excluded. Settings support autosave, JSON and history, and a dedicated CSV exports the breakdown. Estimates use wall-axis lengths and exclude reveals, shaft cladding, bay returns and corner corrections. Unassigned wall spans are flagged. Wallpaper roll counts still depend on roll dimensions and pattern matching.
- Browse 94 furniture presets across 8 categories or search across categories. Add by clicking or dragging, then edit position, size, rotation and color. New types include bunk beds, daybeds, recliners, storage, kitchen appliances, laundry towers, standing desks, exercise bikes and balcony furniture; each has a 2D symbol and 3D model. The sample's two balcony doors are sliding doors. Older sample doors without an explicit style are repaired when their original geometry is intact; explicit user choices are preserved. 3D supports orbit/top views, daylight/night, cut/full-height walls, desktop WASD walkthrough, movable furniture and clickable doors.
- Local autosave, undo/redo, validated JSON import/export, PNG export and CSV material/utility schedules. Original v1 furniture, room finishes and demolished walls are migrated.

## Room planning and space analysis

Open **空间分析** (Space analysis), select a room and use the inspector to set its purpose or apply a suggestion. The local rules use names, furniture, plumbing, shafts, railings and room geometry. Suggestions explain their evidence; weak shape-only guesses remain unconfirmed and manual choices are preserved. Recognition candidates can also be assigned a type before import. Room text OCR is not included.

**一键自动布置** furnishes the selected room using standard or compact catalog items. It fills missing furniture by default; optional replacement removes furniture whose centers belong to that room only after at least one new item fits. Placement checks the room polygon, walls, shafts, pipes, railings, door landings and existing furniture. Chairs face tables, while bedside and coffee tables follow their paired furniture. Items that cannot fit are reported. One undo restores the previous arrangement; all placed items remain editable in 2D and 3D. This is an initial layout, so check circulation, operating clearances and kitchen connections afterward.

The area panel reports net floor, furniture footprint, remaining floor and storage footprint, per room and across counted rooms. Net floor subtracts walls and shafts inside the room boundary while retaining doorway floor. Rotated furniture rectangles are clipped to usable floor and overlaps count once. Rugs, wall-mounted equipment and countertop items do not consume additional floor area. Storage is the footprint of cabinets and similar furniture, a subset of furniture area, not internal capacity. Remaining floor need not be connected. Whole-home totals deduplicate overlapping rooms, and a dedicated CSV includes all four metrics. Existing flooring estimates use room contours minus shafts and may differ where walls lie inside the contour.

Toggle daily (bedrooms to bathroom/living room), housework (kitchen, dining, laundry, balcony) and guest (inferred entrance, living, dining) routes independently. Paths avoid structural and furniture obstacles and show direction and length. Furniture bottlenecks have red markers; disconnected structures or insufficient door clearance are reported without drawing a false straight route. The reference width defaults to 600 mm and is adjustable from 300 to 1200 mm. Doors are assumed open. Grid spacing starts at 150 mm and adapts for large plans; tight turns still need review. Routes do not model door motion, steps or full 3D clearance. Analysis layers appear in 2D and exported PNGs; settings and room types persist in JSON, autosave and undo/redo.

## Correcting recognized room partitions

Before importing image-recognition results, use **修正房间分区** to select a candidate (or click it on the canvas). Drag green vertices or edit their world coordinates, then apply the outline. Merge a neighboring candidate, optionally removing interior detected wall spans and deselecting internal openings while keeping the perimeter. Narrow parallel wall gaps can be bridged up to an editable limit (400 mm by default). The image pixels remain unchanged and merging does not determine whether a real wall can be removed.

Choose a horizontal/vertical split or draw two points on the canvas, then drag the blue endpoints or enter coordinates. The line extends to the room outline; colored previews show the two resulting areas. Confirm to create two independently named/typed candidates, with an optional divider wall. Invalid cuts, holes, disconnected mergers and overlap with other candidates are rejected. Each split part must be at least 0.2 m². Undo candidate edits restores up to 30 changes; normal project undo applies after import. Candidate outline edits do not automatically move separately detected walls.

## Automatic utility layout

In **水电布局** (Utilities), choose all rooms or the selected room, floor or ceiling routing, and the required cold/hot water, drainage, power and low-voltage systems. Default horizontal elevations are 50 mm at floor level and 2600 mm overhead; both are editable. Lights follow the ceiling setting. Drainage stays at a 0 mm floor reference in either mode.

Room types and furniture guide the local templates: entrance switches and lights, rotated bedside outlets, TV/desk sockets and network points, kitchen appliances and sink plumbing, bathroom basin/shower/toilet plumbing, and laundry/balcony services. Unknown room types are skipped. Lighting connects the panel to a switch, then the light. Select existing supply/collection points in the source controls; missing sources receive clearly marked suggested locations. Drainage prefers existing drain stacks or suggests a local connection for each room.

Preview shows colored dashed routes, counts, lengths and unresolved connections without changing the project. Apply is one undoable action; settings and routes survive JSON export and reload. Regeneration updates only the selected rooms and systems, reuses suitable manual points and preserves manual routes. Obsolete generated terminals are removed when room use changes; sources remain available to other rooms. Stale explicitly selected sources are reported and their existing layout is preserved.

Routes use checked orthogonal paths through connected doorways, avoiding walls and shafts. Furniture is ignored for hidden floor/ceiling routing. Unreachable endpoints remain as points with a warning instead of a line through a wall. Moving an attached device updates its route endpoint; manually editing route nodes or dragging a route detaches it from automatic planning. Recheck or regenerate after moving devices. 3D includes vertical connections to devices. CSV separates plan lengths and vertical segments; unlinked manual routes retain plan-only lengths. Shared segments are counted per branch, so totals are not a consolidated purchasing schedule.

This is a planning draft, not a reconstruction of hidden services. It does not calculate loads, conductor sizing, drain slopes, beam penetrations, system separation or full 3D clashes. Verify actual supply points, lintels and overhead clearance before construction planning.

### Distinct devices and air conditioning

Cold/hot water points, sockets, switches and lights now have separate 2D symbols and 3D shapes. Legacy generic water points resolve from their automatic tag or a hot-water name; the inspector allows explicit type changes. Power routes distinguish socket, lighting and AC supply purposes with colors, line styles and CSV names. Choose the purpose before drawing or edit it afterward.

Enable the refrigerant and condensate systems, then choose split or central AC. Bedrooms, living rooms, dining rooms and studies receive indoor endpoints and dedicated power points when power is enabled. Split mode suggests an outdoor unit per room; central mode shares an outdoor unit and places indoor terminals overhead. Suggested outdoor locations lie outside the plan; an existing outdoor unit may be selected. Regeneration updates the selected scope and cleans unused automatic outdoor units while preserving references from other rooms and manual routes. Condensate horizontal segments never exceed the outlet elevation.

Central AC here means a refrigerant-based schematic, not a duct or hydronic design. The refrigerant route represents liquid/gas lines as one group; it does not size or measure both separately, calculate loads or condensate slope, select branch fittings, or validate outdoor installation and wall penetrations. Existing saved five-system selections require manually enabling the new AC systems.

Coordinates use millimetres. All processing and storage stay in the browser; export JSON backups to retain multiple designs. See the [Chinese guide](user-guide.md) for the detailed workflow and architecture.
